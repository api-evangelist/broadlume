# Broadlume

> Broadlume is an all-in-one business platform for the flooring industry, serving 4,000+ retailers and 300+ manufacturers with websites, marketing, and operations software. Products span lead-generating websites, digital advertising and SEO, product catalogs, flooring business software (accounting, inventory, job costing, lead management), and a retail selling system with in-store visualization and e-commerce sample ordering. Broadlume is owned by Cyncly.

This profile is maintained by the API Evangelist network. Broadlume does not publish a public developer API, SDKs, or API documentation at this time; the links below are the company's public web surface.
